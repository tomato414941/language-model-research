# AlgoPerf

Kasimbeg et al., Accelerating neural network training: An analysis of the AlgoPerf competition (arXiv 2502.15015, 2025-02).

- A competition that compares improvements to the training algorithm alone, by the time to reach a target on several tasks on fixed hardware. The first round had 18 submissions from 10 teams.
- In the track where submissions include the search ranges of their settings, Distributed Shampoo came first. It beat Adam and others even when compared in wall-clock time.
- In the track with no tuning at all, a submission based on Schedule Free AdamW came first.
- The top submissions held up well when the tasks changed.

Only the abstract was read.
