# Choosing experiments to accelerate collective discovery

Rzhetsky, Foster, Foster, Evans (PNAS 2015, doi:10.1073/pnas.1509757112).

**The main text could not be retrieved; it was read only through search summaries and Kellogg Insight's write-up.**

It modeled science as a network discovered through experiments. The way most researchers choose is efficient early in a field but slows overall progress once the field matures. It recommends taking more risks and publishing more failures. According to the write-up, the optimal strategy is nearly 10× as efficient as a random strategy at finding 50% of the graph.
