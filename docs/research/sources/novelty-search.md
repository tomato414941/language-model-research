# Abandoning Objectives

Lehman, Stanley, Abandoning Objectives: Evolution through the Search for Novelty Alone (Evolutionary Computation, 2011).

An objective metric can lead a search into dead ends. Ignoring the objective and searching only for novelty of behavior reached the objective in maze and biped-walking tasks with far fewer evaluations than a search guided by the objective.

The abstract and part of the introduction were read. The details of the experiments were not read.
