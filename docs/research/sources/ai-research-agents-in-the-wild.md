# AI-Research Agents in the Wild

AI-Research Agents in the Wild: From GitHub and arXiv to Regularities and Gaps (arXiv 2609.11975, 2026-09).

It sorts 139 public repositories and 101 papers as of 2026-06-10 into nine design lineages and examines regularities such as the cost of iteration. Of six candidates, four receive limited support, one is too broad and one's prediction fails.

Only the abstract was read.
